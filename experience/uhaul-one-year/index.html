Checkpoint 01

A New Road

English is my second language.

And suddenly, I had to use it every single day.

The first weeks were a quiet battle. Not with the job itself, but with the voice inside my head asking whether my English was good enough.

Some days, yes. Some days, less so. But I kept showing up, and slowly, the fear got quieter.

Confidence +1

Checkpoint 02

Finding The Rhythm

You learn the job by doing the job.

There is no shortcut for that.

After the first couple of months, something shifted. The systems made sense. The customers felt less unpredictable. I started to trust myself a little more.

195 shifts worked
1,006 hours on the floor
Countless customers helped

Checkpoint 03

The Hard Part

I need to tell you about the day I thought it was over.

I was moving one of our pick-ups, attached to a trailer. Reversing. I hit the gas instead of the brake.

The truck went back hard. It hit the trailer. Both got damaged.

I stood there in the parking lot knowing exactly what I had done. I told my manager immediately. I did not hide it.

That moment could have ended everything. Instead, it became the moment I learned what accountability really looks like.

I did not lose the job. I earned the lesson.

Accountability unlocked

Checkpoint 04

Checkpoint 05

100%. Top 10. All Of North America.

U-Haul inspectors came through for an internal audit.

Our facility was ready.

Mission Complete 100% Vehicle and trailer inspection

We scored 100% on the cars and trailers inspection. That meant the equipment, the checks, and the program were where they needed to be.

And out of the facilities inspected across Canada and the United States, we ranked in the Top 10.

Not me. Us. That result is the clearest proof of what this team can do when everyone shows up and does their part.
